Overview

Why we publish

Good decisions follow good understanding.

Blackvault intends to publish research on finance, economics and industries, so that our own thinking is tested in the open and useful to partners and readers.

Every publication will have a named author or issuing department, a date, a category and, where applicable, a downloadable PDF. We will not publish placeholder research.

Research areas

What we will cover

01

Financial intelligence

Financial markets, investment intelligence, institutional finance and market intelligence, including equities, bonds, currencies, commodities, valuation, capital structures, private equity and portfolio construction.

02

Economics research

Macroeconomics, African economics, Kenyan economics and global economics, covering growth, inflation, monetary and fiscal policy, public debt, trade, regional integration and the external sector.

03

Sector and industry intelligence

Agriculture, energy, financial services, technology, manufacturing, real estate and infrastructure, mining and resources, and transport and logistics.
