Capital allocation philosophy

How we think about capital.

Capital is a trust to be preserved first and grown with judgement. This is how we intend to approach it.

BlackvaultInstitutional perspective

Eight principles

Our approach to capital

Opportunity identification
We look for opportunities through research, partners and inbound proposals, and record each one so none is chased on impulse.
Capital discipline
Capital is finite and every commitment has an opportunity cost. We commit only where we can add judgement as well as funds.
Risk assessment
We ask what could go wrong, how badly, and whether we could withstand it, before we ask what could go right.
Strategic positioning
We seek positions that strengthen over time through relationships, operating insight and partnerships.
Long-term value creation
We measure success across cycles, and avoid decisions that borrow from the future.
Portfolio diversification
We spread exposure across sectors, geographies and time horizons, so no single outcome decides the whole.
Capital preservation
Protecting what we have comes first. Growth that puts the base at risk is not growth we pursue.
Reinvestment and enterprise development
Returns are reinvested into building durable enterprises, not only into the next transaction.
Note: This page describes how Blackvault intends to allocate capital. It contains no returns, deployed-capital figures or performance history.

Opportunities pipeline

How an opportunity moves through Blackvault

Select a stage to see what happens. Individual opportunities, evaluations and deal documents are confidential and are not published here.

Stage 01

Opportunity identification

Ideas arise from our research, partners and inbound proposals. Each is recorded in an opportunity register with a short description and a source.

Stage 02

Preliminary screening

A fast test against our mandate, risk limits, capital needs and fit. Most opportunities stop here, and that is by design.

Stage 03

Strategic evaluation

Market structure, economics, competition and fit with the group are examined in depth, with explicit assumptions.

Stage 04

Due diligence

Legal, financial and operating review, with verification of counterparties, titles, licences and claims.

Stage 05

Investment or partnership consideration

Structure, terms, approvals and exposure limits are agreed. Nothing proceeds without documented approval.

Stage 06

Execution and development

Capital is deployed in stages against milestones, with clear responsibilities and records.

Stage 07

Monitoring and value creation

Performance is tracked against plan. We decide whether to reinvest, rework or exit, and record what we learned.
